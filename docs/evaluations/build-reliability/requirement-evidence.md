## Validation limits (explicit)

1. **Live observations are bounded.** Twenty real Codex turns were recorded for R-002, R-019 and
   R-022 (thirteen in the initial evaluation and seven in the 2026-09-25 Worker-commit run), with
   provider-reported usage metadata linked in `live-codex/usage-metadata.json`. R-002 used synthetic
   current-contract evidence; no real KV Core endpoint or credential was contacted. The observed
   Work/Unblock behavior is not a deterministic model guarantee. The narrow first contradiction
   fixture returned `remedy_available` because its phase excluded live acceptance; the corrected
   fixture made simultaneous satisfaction of the frozen and current contract a completion condition
   and then produced `blocked` / `external_requirement` as recorded. `claude`, `cursor-agent` and
   `codex` are all installed on this host and their live mappings were observed on 2026-09-25
   (`live-roles/`), including a Codex turn that first failed with `401 Unauthorized` because the
   launching shell had inherited the Claude Code app's proxy environment — with those variables
   removed, Codex works and the criteria pass.
2. **Effective Codex model/effort/permission remain unattested.** Fresh and resumed calls passed the
   exact requested Luna/medium settings and completed, but the provider transport did not attest its
   effective model or reasoning effort. The Worker-commit run is the same: the configured
   `--dangerously-bypass-approvals-and-sandbox` was accepted and the commits landed, which shows the
   mapping reaches the provider and the effective policy permits the work, but not that the flag
   (rather than the host's own `sandbox_mode = "danger-full-access"` default, recorded as a host
